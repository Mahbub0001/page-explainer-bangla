History of Renewable Energy

Renewable energy is energy derived from natural resources that replenish themselves in less time than it takes to consume them. Solar energy, wind energy, and hydroelectric energy are primary forms of renewable power. Solar panels convert sunlight directly into electricity using semiconductor materials.
